Common questions

Can I see prices?

Pricing, lead times and options are quoted per project, because finishes, quantities and customisation change the price. Send your brief and we reply with a quote in baht.

Request the catalogue, then send us the model codes and quantities you need. The codes keep every order unambiguous.

Can you customise sizes, finishes or branding?

Yes. Panels, patterns, top materials, colours and branding can be customised per project, and custom sizes are available on request, especially for buffet stations.
